# Quality Gate

Run this before delivery. Regenerate if any hard rule fails.

- [ ] Final card is portrait 3:4 on ivory paper.
- [ ] Upper source photo is complete, unchanged, and proportionally contained.
- [ ] Existing source packaging remains in the upper photo and is not repeated in watercolor.
- [ ] Lower area reads as a compact horizontal monthly page, never a second poster or large vertical illustration.
- [ ] There is one free-edge watercolor block on the lower left, not a collage or multiple motifs.
- [ ] Watercolor stays left and clear of the footer.
- [ ] Right column is 24–28% wide, unbordered, warm paper, and vertically aligned.
- [ ] Headphone sits above the song; song is lower than the literary block.
- [ ] Book/title is 1.5x quote/handwriting; only month digits are larger.
- [ ] Large blank space before the footer does not exceed 12% of lower-page height.
- [ ] Footer has one thin rule and exact left/center/right copy.
- [ ] Literature and song are verified, or the fallback was used transparently.
- [ ] No date grid, swatches, logo, watermark, badge, sticker, platform UI, or unrequested text appears.
